Welcome! I am a Ph.D. candidate in International Economics and Finance at Brandeis University, where I work under the supervision of Prof. Raphael Schoenle.

My research interests lie in macroeconomics and monetary economics, with a focus on the design of monetary policy under information frictions and non-standard household preferences. My current work studies how sticky information and wealth in the utility function jointly shape the transmission and optimal design of monetary policy.

Before joining Brandeis, I served as Deputy Director of Research at the Ministry of Finance of Mexico and was a Research Fellow at the Center for Economic Studies of El Colegio de México.

I will be available for interviews in the 2026–2027 economics job market.
